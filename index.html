DISCOGRAPHY

Curious where the samples came from? Browse the playlists on my YouTube channel.

RHV2

This project is heavily sample-driven, which is a style of hip-hop I both enjoy listening to and making. It's a sound I've continued to explore ever since.

This started out as a heartbreak album, I actually still have some of the premature lyrics and beats. It was called just red hairs at the time that's why this second iteration is called RHV2.

This version is way more polished and has a more cohesive direction and sound pallete while still employing a diverse collection of samples (which you can find on a playlist on my youtube channel). I wanted to express more of the sounds I enjoy listening to and the emotions I was overwhelmed with at the time. I was planning to include lyrics in this one but I think the samples and instrumentals tell a complete enough story where you can infer what I meant.

I discovered myself as a beatmaker and as a person while making this. I learned a lot about the styles I like, the music I enjoy listening to (which reflected in my sample choices), and the way I want to express myself through music. I also learned a lot about how I like to structure an album.

pestoهات

This is way less emotionally charged than it's predecessor RHV2. I wanted to make something that showcases the way I improved in my craft and experiment with different styles I haven't explored. I also met clockwize while making this album and he helped me a lot with this project and even has production credits on one of the tracks.

Although this project doesn't have a lot of emotional relevance to me but I don't think each project has to be an emotional deconstruction of myself. The beats are still sick and they sound really cool. Some of my most polished and grand sounding work is on here along with some of my most minimal sounding beats which creates a nice contrast in my opinion.

اغاني مش هتعجب ابوك

The very confrontational title was inspired by my actual father. He doesn't really like hip-hop and especially the way I make beats. So I thought by making something he'd absolutely hate I'd be making something that was true to myself.

This one is in between RHV2 and pestoهات in terms of emotional relevance. I turned twenty and I was trying to wrap my head around the fact that I was no longer a teenager and that things are getting real now. This project can be thought of as a last grasp at the teenage rebelious spirit I always thought I had.

I think this is me at my most experimental. If throwing things at the wall 'till it sticks was an album it would be this. I tried mixing different genres together with hip-hop and used a way more diverse collection of sounds, samples, and techniques.

This is the first time I didn't just dump the entire project into a single video and uploaded each track as a separate video which I think is a much better way to showcase my projects in the future.
